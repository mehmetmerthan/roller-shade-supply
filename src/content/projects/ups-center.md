## Overview

A new build-out at UPS Center added private offices, a meeting room, and a break room alongside the facility's warehouse floor. Several of the rooms look directly onto the sorting floor through interior windows.

## The Challenge

Staff needed privacy and fewer distractions from the activity on the warehouse floor, the break room's exterior windows took strong afternoon sun, and each area was finished on a different schedule.

## Our Solution

Interior office and meeting-room windows received gray textured roller shades, inside-mounted with a white fascia for a finished look. The break room received dark gray room-darkening shades on its exterior windows. Every shade is chain-operated, and orders were delivered area by area as the build-out was completed.

## The Result

Each room can close itself off from the warehouse floor in seconds, and the break room stays comfortable through bright afternoons.
