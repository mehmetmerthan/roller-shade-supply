## Overview

Stern Pinball's conference rooms sit behind full-height glass walls that look out over an open office. The glass keeps the building open and connected, but the rooms also need to close off for meetings and presentations.

## The Challenge

Each glass wall spans several shade widths, and shades that rise and fall one at a time look uneven and take too long before a meeting starts. The work also had to happen outside production hours.

## Our Solution

We supplied motorized roller shades in a white room-darkening fabric, grouped so every shade on a wall raises and lowers together from a single control. The shades stack neatly at the ceiling line when open, leaving the full glass clear. Installation was scheduled over two weekends.

## The Result

Each conference room switches from open to private with one touch, and the shades move as one clean surface across the glass.
