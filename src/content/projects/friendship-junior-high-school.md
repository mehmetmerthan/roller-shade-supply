## Overview

Friendship Junior High School renovated its classrooms, media center, administrative offices, and multipurpose room. The window coverings had to be finished before students returned in the fall.

## The Challenge

The schedule was fixed by the school calendar, the project was union, and the district required submittals and documentation before anything could be ordered.

## Our Solution

We prepared product submittals early, then supplied white light-filtering roller shades for the classrooms, set under a continuous black fascia that runs the full length of each ribbon of windows. The media center and multipurpose room received blackout fabric for projectors and presentations. Deliveries were sequenced room by room with the contractor's summer schedule.

## The Result

Every room was complete before the first day of school, with documentation delivered for the district's records.
