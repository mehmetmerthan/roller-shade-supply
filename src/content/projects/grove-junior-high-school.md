## Overview

Grove Junior High School replaced the window coverings in its classrooms, library, and gymnasium as part of a phased modernization. The work was split over two summers, one wing at a time.

## The Challenge

Each phase had to be fully closed out before students returned in the fall. The library and gymnasium have tall windows that bring harsh glare onto screens and the gym floor, while classrooms needed dependable everyday light control.

## Our Solution

Classrooms received chain-operated light-filtering roller shades. The library and gymnasium received solar screen roller shades that cut glare while keeping daylight and the view, with chain lengths and tension devices sized for the taller windows. Deliveries were sequenced wing by wing with the contractor's summer schedule.

## The Result

Both phases closed out on schedule, and the school now has one consistent window covering standard across every wing.
