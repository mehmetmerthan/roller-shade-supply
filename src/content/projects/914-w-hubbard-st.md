## Overview

914 W Hubbard St is a condo building in River West with large windows facing the skyline and treetops. The property manager wanted one standard window covering for units as they turned over, and a simple way to reorder later.

## The Challenge

Units had a mix of aging window coverings, window sizes varied from unit to unit, and the work had to happen in vacant units without disturbing occupied neighbors.

## Our Solution

We documented each unit's window sizes and standardized on chain-operated roller shades with white rounded cassettes. Living rooms and bedrooms received a white room-darkening fabric, side windows received a light-filtering fabric, and kitchens received a solar screen that keeps the view. Shades were installed unit by unit as each condo turned over.

## The Result

Every renovated unit now has the same clean look, and the property manager can reorder for future turnovers with a single email.
