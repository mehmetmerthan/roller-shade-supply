## Overview

This renovation covered a community club's main hall, a wood-beamed social hall, a second lounge, and a smaller side room. The goal was a calmer, more comfortable space through the day without losing natural light.

## The Challenge

Low afternoon sun came straight through the windows while the halls were in use. The social hall's long ribbon of windows needed to read as one clean line, and the building had to stay open for club events while the work was done.

## Our Solution

We supplied white solar screen roller shades that cut glare while keeping the view and daylight. In the main hall they're inside-mounted in each double-hung window; in the social hall they run as a continuous band across the ribbon windows. The side room received charcoal room-darkening shades. All shades are chain-operated with tension devices, and installation was scheduled in two phases around the club's events.

## The Result

The main hall and lounges stay comfortable through bright afternoons, every room shares one consistent look, and the building never had to close for the work.
