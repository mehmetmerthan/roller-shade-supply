## Overview

Grove Junior High School replaced the window coverings in its classrooms, library, and gymnasium as part of a phased modernization. The work was split over two summers, one wing at a time.

## The Challenge

The tall windows in the library and gymnasium couldn't be operated safely by hand, and each phase had to be fully closed out before students returned in the fall.

## Our Solution

Classrooms received chain-operated light-filtering roller shades. The library and gymnasium received motorized 5% solar screen shades on wall controls, which cut glare while keeping daylight and the view.

## The Result

Both phases closed out on schedule, and staff can now control glare in the library and gym with the press of a switch.
