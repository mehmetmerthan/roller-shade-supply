## Overview

Water Power Lofts Rockford converted an industrial building into loft apartments with exposed concrete, open ductwork, and tall steel-grid windows. The general contractor needed window coverings that would look uniform from the street, give residents real light control, and arrive on the same schedule as the floors were turned over.

## The Challenge

The large steel-grid openings were too wide for a single shade, unit layouts varied, and floors were finished at different times. Any mix-up between units would mean delays at final inspection.

## Our Solution

We split each large opening into two or more chain-operated roller shades, mounted at the head of the masonry openings, in one gray solar screen fabric throughout. The screen controls glare while keeping the view, and the open-roll hardware stays quiet against the steel windows. Every shade was labeled by unit and room and delivered in three phases tied to the contractor's turnover schedule.

## The Result

All 248 shades arrived on schedule with no unit mix-ups, and the steel-grid windows read as one consistent facade by day and at night.
