## Overview

811 West Evergreen is a brick loft building on Chicago's Near North Side, rehabbed into apartments with exposed ductwork, new black-framed windows, and tall balcony doors.

## The Challenge

Each balcony door and its transom needed a single full-height shade that wouldn't get in the way of the door hardware. The new windows sat in deep drywall returns, and units were finished in batches.

## Our Solution

Each opening was measured individually, and we supplied chain-operated roller shades in a white solar screen, inside-mounted at the head of each return with a fascia for a clean finish. On the balcony doors, one shade covers both the door and its transom. Shades were delivered in two batches, labeled by unit, as the units were finished.

## The Result

Every unit has the same clean, bright look, with views kept through the screen fabric and nothing in the way of the doors.
