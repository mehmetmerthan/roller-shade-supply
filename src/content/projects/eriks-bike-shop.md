## Overview

The ERIK'S Bike Shop store has a tall glass window wall along its storefront and a mezzanine that overlooks the sales floor.

## The Challenge

Afternoon sun through the tall glass brought glare and heat onto the sales floor and could fade merchandise, but the store needed its displays to stay visible from the parking lot.

## Our Solution

We supplied oversized roller shades in a charcoal solar screen for the window wall, with each bay sized individually. The screen cuts glare and heat while keeping the view in both directions, and the dark color recedes behind the glass so the storefront still reads as open.

## The Result

The sales floor stays comfortable through bright afternoons, and the bikes stay on display.
