## Overview

SRIS International School renovated a set of classrooms, offices, and a brick-walled multipurpose room. Many of the rooms are connected by interior window walls that keep the building open and bright.

## The Challenge

Glass between rooms is good for sightlines, but it makes it hard to focus, present, or hold a private meeting. Each room needed a simple way to close off its windows, and everything had to be ready before the school year began.

## Our Solution

We supplied light gray roller shades in a room-darkening fabric for every interior window wall, chain-operated so teachers and staff can raise or lower them in seconds. In the multipurpose room the shades are inside-mounted at the head of the frames; in the smaller rooms they're mounted on the wall above the frames.

## The Result

Each room can switch from open to private in seconds, and the work was finished two weeks before the first day of school.
