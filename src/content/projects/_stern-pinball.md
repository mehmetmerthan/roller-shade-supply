## Overview

Stern Pinball needed window coverings for its lobby, private offices, and production areas, where large windows bring in strong daylight.

## The Challenge

The highest windows had no power nearby, and opening finished walls to run wiring wasn't an option. Work also had to fit around production shifts.

## Our Solution

Solar-powered motorized shades handled the high windows without any new wiring: a small panel keeps each motor's battery charged. The lobby and production areas received 5% solar screen roller shades, and private offices received zebra shades for adjustable privacy.

## The Result

The high windows are now controlled by remote, and the work finished without interrupting production.
