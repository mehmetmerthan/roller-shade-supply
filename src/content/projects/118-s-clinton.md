## Overview

118 S Clinton is a loft building in Chicago's West Loop, with storefronts at street level and large multi-pane windows on the floors above. This project covered the building's shared amenity and leasing spaces, where tall windows look out on neighboring towers.

## The Challenge

The street-facing windows take strong afternoon sun, but the city view is a big part of what these spaces offer. The shades also had to fit beneath exposed ductwork without looking like an afterthought.

## Our Solution

We supplied white solar screen roller shades, inside-mounted at the window heads beneath the ductwork, with clean headboxes across each opening. The screen fabric cuts glare and heat while keeping the skyline in view, and one fabric color throughout keeps every window consistent.

## The Result

The spaces stay comfortable through bright afternoons without losing their view, and the shades sit quietly within the building's loft character.
