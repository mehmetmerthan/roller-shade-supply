## Overview

Lexus Glenview is a dealership with a glass-walled showroom, a customer lounge, private sales offices, and a conference room.

## The Challenge

The tall showroom glass brought strong glare onto vehicles, screens, and customers, the conference room needed quick darkening for presentations, and the reopening date was fixed.

## Our Solution

The showroom and lounge received oversized solar screen roller shades with chain tension devices, which control glare while keeping the vehicles visible from the street. The sales offices received light-filtering shades, and the conference room received a blackout shade for presentations. Every order was sized individually from the window schedule.

## The Result

The dealership reopened on schedule with a showroom that stays comfortable all day and vehicles that stay on display behind the glass.
