## Overview

Salt Creek Elementary School needed new window coverings in its classrooms, library, gym, and offices. The district wanted a dependable product that fit a fixed budget and would hold up to years of daily use.

## The Challenge

The budget left little room for options, but the product still had to be durable, safe around young students, and consistent from room to room.

## Our Solution

We standardized on chain-operated roller shades with tension devices and a single light-filtering fabric, which kept pricing predictable and simplified ordering. Standard sizes were used wherever the window schedule allowed.

## The Result

The school received a durable, uniform window covering package within budget, delivered and complete well before the first day of classes.
