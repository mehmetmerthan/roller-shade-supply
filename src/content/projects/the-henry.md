## Overview

We installed roller shades across 245 luxury residential units and 49 townhouses, with many featuring motorized operation and solar charging for effortless light control.

## The Challenge

A new community of this size needed one consistent look on every window, from the apartment buildings to the townhouses, and shades that residents could operate easily on tall windows across open living spaces.

## Our Solution

We supplied cream light-filtering roller shades throughout, with motorized operation and solar charging on many of them, so residents can adjust daylight without cords and without hardwiring each shade.

## The Result

Every residence shares the same clean shade line inside and from the street, and residents control their light with a touch.
