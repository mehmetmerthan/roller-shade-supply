## Overview

Lexus Glenview is a dealership with a glass-walled showroom, a customer lounge, private sales offices, and a conference room.

## The Challenge

The showroom glass brought strong glare onto vehicles and screens, the conference room needed quick darkening for presentations, and the reopening date was fixed.

## Our Solution

The showroom and lounge received 3% solar screen roller shades on motors, which control glare while keeping the vehicles visible from the street. The conference room received a blackout roller shade, and the sales offices received cordless zebra shades for adjustable privacy.

## The Result

The dealership reopened on schedule with a showroom that stays comfortable all day and one-touch light control on the tall glass.
